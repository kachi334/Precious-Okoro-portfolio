The Thinking Behind The Writing

Good content starts long before the first draft.

I approach writing with an understanding of the product, the people using it, and the business behind it. That means looking beyond keywords and word counts to understand what an audience needs to know, what questions a product needs to answer, and what a business needs its content to accomplish.

With over six years of experience across content design, strategy, editorial work, and product communications, I bring a broader perspective to the writing process.

I don't just think about what needs to be written. I think about why it needs to exist, where it fits, and what it should make possible.
