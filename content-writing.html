Why Good Content Matters

Your product is moving. Your content needs to keep up.

As technology evolves, companies have more to explain: new capabilities, changing customer expectations, complex use cases, and increasingly competitive markets. I help close the gap between what your product does, what your audience understands, and what your business needs them to do.

When your product is difficult to explain

Your team understands the technology. Your audience still needs to understand why it matters. I turn product knowledge into accessible, compelling content.

When your expertise isn't translating into visibility

Your team has valuable ideas, but they're not reaching the people who need to hear them. I turn that expertise into useful, discoverable editorial content.

When content attracts attention but doesn't build confidence

Traffic alone isn't the goal. Your content needs to answer meaningful questions, demonstrate value, and give potential customers reasons to trust your business.

Be Found. Be Useful. Be Trusted.

Content should be worth finding.

People discover brands, compare products, and research unfamiliar subjects across search engines, answer engines, and AI-powered platforms. I bring search strategy into the editorial process, balancing discoverability with substance, usefulness, and reader experience.

SEO: Search Engine Optimization

Keyword and search-intent research, on-page optimization, topic clusters, internal linking, and content gap analysis.

AEO: Answer Engine Optimization

Question-led content, useful direct answers, structured explanations, and content designed to make information easier to retrieve.

GEO: Generative Engine Optimization

Research-backed content, topical depth, entity relevance, clear sourcing, and original insights that strengthen visibility across AI-powered discovery.

The goal isn't to write for an algorithm at the expense of a reader. It's to create content that deserves to be discovered, cited, remembered, and trusted.

How I Work

Research first. Writing follows.

Good content starts well before the first draft. I take the time to understand the subject, the audience, and what the work needs to achieve.

01

Understand

I get to know the product, audience, industry, competitive landscape, and business objectives. I work with subject-matter experts where the subject demands it.

02

Find the angle

I identify the questions worth answering, the story worth telling, and the most useful way to approach it. This includes search intent, positioning, and the content's role in the customer journey.

03

Develop

I turn research and strategy into clear, engaging, well-structured content. Every claim has a reason to be there. Every section has a job.

04

Refine

I work through feedback, editorial review, and optimization to make sure the final piece is accurate, useful, and ready for its intended audience.

Before We Work Together

A quick fit check.

I take on a limited number of clients at a time, so it is worth being direct about who this is for.

Good fit

  • ✓You are a B2B SaaS or technology company with a product worth explaining well.
  • ✓You want content informed by product thinking, not just keywords.
  • ✓You need one senior writer across strategy and execution, not a volume mill.
  • ✓You are building thought leadership, not just chasing search traffic.
  • ✓You would rather approve a draft than rewrite one from scratch.

Probably not a fit

  • ✕You need high-volume content at the lowest possible cost.
  • ✕You want keyword-stuffed posts with no product understanding behind them.
  • ✕You need a finished article live by tomorrow morning.
  • ✕You want a writer who takes instructions without weighing in on strategy.
