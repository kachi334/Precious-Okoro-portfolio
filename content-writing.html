How I Work With Teams

Built for distributed teams

01 · Discovery

I start with your reader, not your brief

A call or an async doc. I ask who's actually reading this and what they need to believe before they act.

02 · Drafting

I write in your voice, not mine

I work from a style guide if you have one, and build one from your existing content if you don't.

Comments in your docs, not a meeting you have to stay up for. I've worked this way with teams five time zones away.

04 · Delivery

On deadline, in your format

Docs, CMS, Figma, markdown. I ship it where your team actually works.
