When This Usually Happens

The moment content stops keeping up.

A product rarely outgrows its content all at once. It happens at specific moments, and most teams only notice once the gap is already costing them.

A feature ships ahead of the words

Engineering moves faster than the explanation. Sales is improvising, and prospects are left to guess at the value.

AI enters the product

The content now has to build trust in a system, not just describe a feature. That takes more than a changelog entry.

Content needs to scale, the team does not

The backlog grows faster than anyone has time to write for it, and there is no budget for a full-time hire.

This is where the writing comes in.

How I Work

Research first. Writing follows.

01

Research

Understand the audience, product, industry, competitive landscape, and questions that matter. The brief reflects what your audience actually needs to know, not assumptions.

02

Strategy

Define the content's purpose, positioning, search intent, editorial angle, and intended outcome. Every piece has a clear job before a word gets written.

03

Execution

Translate complex information into useful, engaging, well-structured content. The result is usable, not just published.

04

Systems

Ensure content contributes to a consistent communication experience and supports broader business objectives. Results compound instead of resetting with every new piece.

Before We Work Together

A quick fit check.

I take on a limited number of clients at a time, so it is worth being direct about who this is for.

Good fit

  • ✓You are a B2B SaaS or technology company with a product worth explaining well.
  • ✓You want content informed by product thinking, not just keywords.
  • ✓You need one senior writer across strategy and execution, not a volume mill.
  • ✓You are building thought leadership, not just chasing search traffic.
  • ✓You would rather approve a draft than rewrite one from scratch.

Probably not a fit

  • ✕You need high-volume content at the lowest possible cost.
  • ✕You want keyword-stuffed posts with no product understanding behind them.
  • ✕You need a finished article live by tomorrow morning.
  • ✕You want a writer who takes instructions without weighing in on strategy.
