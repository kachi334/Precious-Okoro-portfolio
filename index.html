02 / How I Think
01

Language is infrastructure.

I don't think of content as something added to an interface after the product is designed. Language shapes what people understand, what they expect, what they can do, and how the system responds.

02

I think in systems.

I look beyond individual screens to patterns, states, edge cases, governance, and the structures that make content scalable.

03

Intelligent products change the job.

When software starts planning, deciding, and acting, content design expands beyond conversation. It becomes about intent, delegation, transparency, confidence, recovery, and human control.
