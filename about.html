About

Hi, I'm Precious. I build the language infrastructure behind products

I'm a Senior Content Strategist and Content Designer with 6+ years of experience helping B2B SaaS, fintech, edtech, and enterprise technology teams build content that drives product adoption, retention, and growth.

Since 2019, I've worked across independent and in-house environments, designing the content systems, governance models, and workflows that make that infrastructure hold together, so teams can create clear, consistent, user-centered experiences at scale.

My work sits at the intersection of content strategy, UX writing, information architecture, and product thinking. I don't just write words. I build the systems, processes, and content decisions that help products communicate better, remove friction, and move users toward meaningful outcomes.

Increasingly, that means designing for agents instead of screens. I think about how an agent should behave, what knowledge and context it needs to answer well, how its responses are structured, and where a system needs guardrails to stay trustworthy under real use. Voxra, the AI content system I'm currently building to govern how agents communicate, is where I'm putting that thinking into practice.

Who I work best with

I work best with founders and product teams who treat language as a system, not a finishing touch. If you want someone who challenges an unclear decision instead of writing around it, we'll work well together.
